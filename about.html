About

Jabbar Abdurrahman

Founder, Hadnet Innovations

Background

Jabbar has spent more than 20 years in cruise and travel, where the work he enjoyed most was always the project inside the job: building teams, training people across countries and hitting milestones.

As a Retail Travel Sales Manager, he led a team of eight that reached its first major milestone within three months and helped grow seasonal sales by 28%. During a Sabre booking system rollout, he served as the training link between teams in the US and Abu Dhabi, including a month on site in Abu Dhabi.

He founded Hadnet Innovations to bring that same discipline, steady and deliberate iteration, to hard engineering problems.

Credentials

  • Six Sigma Yellow Belt (2026)
  • CLIA Certified Cruise Counselor
  • Studying for the CAPM, on the path to the PMP

Why "Hadnet"

Hadnet is the Tigrinya word for unity. The logo's six points joined through one copper center reflect it: if one link breaks, the rest still hold.
