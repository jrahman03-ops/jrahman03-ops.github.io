Project AETHER-3

What if the quietest place to listen for dark matter and neutrinos is a permanently shadowed crater on the Moon?

The idea

AETHER-3 imagines three octagonal stations in a permanently shadowed lunar crater, working together as a dual dark matter and neutrino detector. It is speculative and likely decades away. It is also where Hadnet's octagonal design thinking began.

Status

Stage
Speculative concept. Not built or tested.
