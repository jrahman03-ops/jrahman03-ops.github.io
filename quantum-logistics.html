Quantum Logistics

Quantum hardware is fragile, temperature-sensitive and new to most supply chains. We help organizations get ready to move it.

The problem

Quantum processors, cryostats and cryogenic supplies are sensitive to temperature, vibration and handling. Most freight, port and storage teams have never moved them, and makers, carriers and receivers often plan in separate silos.

What we offer

Readiness audit

A structured review of how prepared your operation is to receive, store and move quantum and cryogenic hardware, with a clear list of gaps.

Coordination planning

A shared plan that lines up makers, carriers, storage and receiving teams around one timeline and one set of handling rules.

Who it's for

  • Logistics and freight companies preparing for high-value technical cargo
  • Port, warehouse and cold-chain operators
  • Research labs and companies bringing quantum hardware on site
